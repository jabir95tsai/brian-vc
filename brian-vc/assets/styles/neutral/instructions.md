# Neutral VC style

Apply this style only after the analytical content package is frozen.

- Preserve every required section, source, warning, and data-quality label.
- Use a restrained white background, dark text, one muted accent color, and no branding.
- Prefer tables for comparable numeric evidence and prose for judgments.
- Keep units, periods, source dates, and confidence labels adjacent to each number.
- Keep the executive and full-critical versions visually related but content-distinct.
- Do not hide missing data, red flags, dissenting analysis, or failed validation.
- Do not change calculations, rankings, wording, or evidence merely to fit a layout.
- Paginate long tables instead of deleting rows or shrinking text below a readable size.
- Render with the platform's available document, spreadsheet, or presentation workflow.
